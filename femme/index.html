Why do I feel so bloated before my period even though I eat clean?
✨
Totally normal — and it's not your food. In late luteal (days 22–28), progesterone drops sharply and your body retains water as a result. It's a hormonal response, not a diet failure.

What actually helps: reduce sodium slightly, increase magnesium (400mg), and add gentle movement like walking or yoga. It usually resolves within 2 days of your period starting.
